Portico — Desk Reference

Check-In, Active Patrons & Departures, at a glance

Three laminate-ready cards for the screens staff live in most on a shift: the check-in desk, the floor roster, and logging who's left. Each covers the golden path plus the exceptions that actually come up — watchlist notes, capacity, guests, behavior notes. Print each card on its own sheet.

Each card breaks to its own page.

Card 1 of 3

Check-In

/admin/check-in

Who works this screen: ShowrunnerVolunteerDM Door +ManagerAdminOwner

  1. Find the memberSearch by the fields your club has set up — the "How to check someone in" panel on the screen lists them.

  2. Read the status lineIt shows the instant they're selected, before any event. Green = go. Amber = do one thing first. Red = stop and get a manager.

  3. Clear anything amber"Save & promote to Irregular" for a Prospective, "Confirm paperwork on file" for missing paperwork — both sit right under the member's name.

  4. Pick tonight's eventShows the Due total. Most walk-ins need nothing else — Payment options (subscription, add-ons, voucher, comp) stays folded until you need it.

  5. Take payment, click Check inChoose how they paid in the dialog and confirm. Done.

Do not admit

Banned or deceased members can't be checked in — no override here. A granted one-event exception shows as amber instead, not red.

Watchlist

Notify the staff channel first, then tick the acknowledgment in the Check in dialog — it won't submit without it.

Under age

"Check ID — under 21, no alcohol, mark hand": they're admitted, but ID and mark them. (The age is set by the club.)

Prepaid

Already paid or on the comp list? The screen says "Prepaid — not yet arrived". Click Mark arrived instead of Check in — nothing more to collect.

No event needed

Selling a subscription or a day pass is its own sale — open "Sell a subscription or day pass" under the member's name. It doesn't check anyone in.

Guests

"Register a guest" appears once the host is checked in tonight, isn't on probation, and hasn't reached the club's guest limit for the night. Collects username, preferred, first and last name, and email; date of birth only if you tick "Appears to be under 21".

At capacity

Check in is replaced by an "At capacity" message once the building is full. Log a departure (Active Patrons, or Record Departures on the Dashboard) and it comes back.

Left by mistake

Departed someone who's still here? Select them and click Mark as returned — no new check-in, no new charge.

Practice

"Enter training mode" at the top of the screen lets you rehearse the whole flow. Nothing is saved until you exit it.

Cash box

If your desk uses one, pick the Register and click Open Box at the start of the night. Drops, other payments and Close Box are under "Cash box".

Showrunner⊂ Volunteer⊂ DM⊂ Door⊂ Manager⊂ Admin⊂ Owner

Filled rung = minimum role for this screen. Everything above it can use it too. A club may display these under different names (see Role Labels) — the ranking is unchanged.

Active Patrons

/admin/active-patrons

Who works this screen: ShowrunnerVolunteer + DMDoor ManagerAdminOwner

  1. Read the roomEveryone checked in and not yet departed, across every event running right now — not just one.

  2. Depart people as they leaveClick a username, or its Depart button. Capacity frees up immediately for the next walk-in.

  3. Leave a visit noteTemporary — e.g. a clothing description to help spot someone tonight. Gone once the event's over.

  4. Leave a behavior notePermanent, on the member's record — for anything staff should know later, not just tonight.

  5. Watch the watchlistToggle "Watchlist only" to filter the room down to flagged patrons.

Overnight

A guest staying over (an add-on flagged Overnight, e.g. private room/sleepover) keeps showing here past their event's window, until someone departs them.

Who sees what

Behavior notes: you always see your own in full. Volunteer sees a count of others'. DM and Door see every note's text. Manager+ also sees who wrote it.

Departed by mistake

There's no undo here. At the Check-In desk (Door+), select the member and tonight's event, then click Mark as returned.

Also in the building

The header lists signed-in Volunteer+ staff too — not just patrons — so you can tell who else is on site.

Record Departures

/admin (Dashboard)

Who works this screen: ShowrunnerVolunteer + DMDoor ManagerAdminOwner

  1. Open Record DeparturesA widget right on the Dashboard — the first thing you see after logging in.

  2. Enter a headcountHow many people left without a formal checkout. A reason is optional.

  3. SubmitTonight's occupancy count drops immediately, freeing up room for the next walk-in.

Anonymous

This is a headcount, not a per-person record — use it when you know people left but not exactly who. To depart one specific, known person instead, use Active Patrons.

Capacity only

This doesn't touch any individual attendance row — it only adjusts the building's occupancy count against its capacity limit, if one's set.
